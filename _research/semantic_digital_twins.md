This research develops semantic digital twins that connect technical documents, system models, and evolving project knowledge for adaptive infrastructure planning.

## Technical innovation

The approach combines semantic modeling with language-model reasoning so a digital twin can interpret knowledge-intensive planning questions, connect evidence across sources, and retain traceability to the underlying project context.

## Practical insight

Infrastructure programs depend on knowledge scattered across contracts, standards, reports, and stakeholder decisions. The framework supports more transparent planning when requirements, evidence, and priorities change over time.
