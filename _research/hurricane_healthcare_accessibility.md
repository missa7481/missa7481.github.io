This collaborative UMD–NYU project examines how Hurricane Helene disrupted access to healthcare and other essential services, with particular attention to uneven impacts across communities.

## Technical innovation

The project integrates real-time travel data, hurricane-severity measures, county-level social-media sentiment, and sociodemographic information to connect environmental hazards and public response with post-hurricane mobility disruption.

## Practical insight

The analysis identifies disproportionate mobility declines among lower-income, rural, and Black communities. These findings support more equitable emergency management, healthcare-access planning, and community-resilience strategies during hurricane events.

## Collaboration

Collaborative research conducted at the University of Maryland and New York University, 2025–2026.
