This funded research project develops evidence-based ways to evaluate risk-management performance in major transportation projects.

## Technical innovation

The work derives data-driven metrics from historical transportation-project annual reports to identify the types of risk encountered by project managers and evaluate how those risks are managed over time.

## Practical insight

The resulting measures help agencies assess project-management performance consistently and translate lessons from prior projects into more informed risk-management practice.

## Sponsorship

Conducted at the University of Maryland and the Build America Center, with support from the U.S. Department of Transportation and Federal Highway Administration, 2022–2023.
